# Service visual system

Managers use this dashboard on the restaurant floor under warm dining-room light; administrators review it on a desktop. Match the user's warm translucent reference with a softly visible restaurant interior, frosted panels, dark brown text, restrained terracotta charts, sage completion states and a compact floating pill dock.

DM Sans for controls, Manrope for headings and metrics. Cards use 16px radii, panel 25px, pill controls and a thin translucent border. Desktop has a slim icon rail and a two-column chart area; mobile removes the rail, scrolls report tabs and tables, and stacks forms and charts. Dense form fields have near-solid backgrounds. Statuses always carry text; focus is visible and reduced motion is supported.
